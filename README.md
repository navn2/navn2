### 🚀 About Me

I'm a 3rd-year engineering student who connects weird components, writes high-performance code, and makes complex systems work. Operating at the intersection of AI, electronics, and architecture, I teach circuits to behave and ideas to come alive. 

*   **Low-Level & Hardware:** RTOS development in QNX Neutrino, real-time control systems via STM32 and ESP32.
*   **Intelligent Systems:** YOLOv8-powered surveillance pipelines and CNN-based RF signal classification.
*   **Software Architecture:** Architecting **Aether-note**, a local-first, markdown-powered native Windows application.

My focus remains the same: engineering technology that refuses to stay ordinary.
